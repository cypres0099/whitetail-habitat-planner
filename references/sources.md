## How to resolve a conflict

Use current MDC rules for legality. For selection and cutting practices, use local soils, flora, natural communities and agency guidance. Use LaPratt for hypotheses about layout and design, then record observations and compare outcomes before scaling. Discount fixed deer ages, antler guarantees, exact rut dates, specific seed products and claims about travel distance unless independently supported for the task.
